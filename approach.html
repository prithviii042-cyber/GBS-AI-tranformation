Our North Star: a System-Governed enterprise

The strategy is anchored on a rigorous operating-model framework. RCPL's target is the System-Governed ("system-driven") archetype — governed autonomy, where intelligent systems initiate work at scale under strong governance. The 12–15 month transformation is the journey up the agency ladder to reach it.

01 · Strategic Foundation

The operating model — and why System-Governed

An operating model is a coherent set of choices, not a wish. We use a proven design framework: five design levers combine into one of five archetypes, supported by six readiness dimensions and a five-level agency ladder. For RCPL, the target is A4 — System-Governed Design. Click an archetype to compare.

From human-initiated to governed autonomy

"Agency" is how far work is initiated by systems rather than people. System-Governed is the only archetype that reaches Level 5. We climb the ladder wave by wave — never faster than governance and data can sustain. Click a level.
