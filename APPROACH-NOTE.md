# Approach Note — Preparing Our Response to RCPL RFP TR/2026/002
### AI-Enabled Enterprise Transformation · 12–15 Month Integrated Engagement

**Purpose:** This note sets out *how we prepare the bid* — the win strategy, response
structure, evaluation-driven priorities, content plan, roles, and timeline. It is an
internal bid-preparation document, not the proposal itself.

---

## 1. What RCPL is actually buying

Read past the document and the client is buying **certainty of outcome at an aggressive velocity**:

- A **fully operational** AI-enabled enterprise by Month 15 — not a strategy, a running SSC + AI factory + modernised ERP.
- **One accountable partner** across *both* Process/SSC and Digital/AI. They have explicitly ruled out multi-vendor.
- **Proof they can trust the timeline.** "9–15 months is non-negotiable" appears twice. They fear ramp-up delay, coordination drag, and rework.
- **Independence at the end.** They want capability transferred so they run it themselves (consultant advisory-only post-M15).

**Implication for the bid:** we win on *credible velocity + integrated depth + de-risked delivery*, not on breadth of ideas. Every section must answer "how do you deliver this fast, together, without slipping?"

---

## 2. Win themes (the 3 messages every section must reinforce)

| # | Win theme | Why it wins here |
|---|-----------|------------------|
| **T1** | **One firm, both streams, single accountability** | Directly answers their single-vendor mandate and removes the 2–3 month coordination drag they call out. |
| **T2** | **Design-first + parallel waves = the timeline is real** | Converts "we can do 12–15 months" (claim) into "here is exactly how" (evidence). This is the credibility battleground. |
| **T3** | **Pre-vetted, named, on-site pods from Day 1** | Kills their #1 fear — ramp-up delay. Names and CVs, not "to be staffed." |
| **T4** | **Built to hand over** | Mixed-team model + KT gates + tapering to advisory. Aligns with their end-state independence requirement. |

Everything we write threads these four. If a paragraph doesn't advance one of them, cut it.

## 4. Evaluation-driven priorities (spend effort where the marks are)

Technical = **60%** of total, Commercial = **40%**. Within the technical 60%, weights are:

| Criterion | Weight | Where we prove it | Bid effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Integrated capability (process/SSC **AND** digital/AI) | **25%** | Case studies + team CVs + engagement model | **Highest** |
| 12–15 month delivery plan (credibility, risk, parallel exec, governance) | **20%** | Integrated delivery plan | **Highest** |
| AI/automation depth (GenAI, agentic, 100+ factory) | 15% | Part B + use-case factory annexe | High |
| Team strength (partner involvement, named leads, on-site) | 15% | Team composition | High |
| Reference quality (recent FMCG India, integrated, velocity) | 15% | Case studies | High |
| Methodology, tools, accelerators | 10% | Cross-cutting in both approaches | Medium |

**So: 45% of the technical score rides on integrated capability + the delivery plan.** These two get our best writers, most senior review, and the strongest evidence. Reference quality + AI depth + team each carry 15% and must clear the bar convincingly, but don't over-invest beyond credible.

**Scoring insight:** "Integrated capability" is worth the most *and* is where single-firm bidders differentiate from consortiums. Lead the exec summary with it and prove it with a case study that shows process + digital delivered *together* in one programme.

## 8. Bid risks & mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **Timeline claim reads as unsubstantiated** | Make the delivery plan *concrete* — named phases, overlapping waves, dependency map, weekly PMO + bi-weekly board cadence. Show, don't assert. |
| **Case studies not recent / not Indian FMCG / not integrated** | Screen hard against all three tests early; if weak, this is the biggest score risk (15% + underpins the 25%). |
| **CVs say "to be staffed"** | Confirm real named people + availability before submission; on-site commitment in writing. |
| **Volume 1 and Volume 2 numbers don't reconcile** | Single source of truth for the business case; finance + solution leads co-own. |
| **Over-writing / blowing page limits** | Bid Manager enforces the page plan from storyboard stage; every page earns its place against a win theme. |
| **Generic "AI slideware"** | Prove depth: agentic + GenAI use cases mapped to real RCPL processes, with value sizing and a factory operating model — not a logo soup of tools. |

## 9. Differentiators to hammer (vs a likely Big-4 / SI field)

- **Genuinely single-firm** integrated delivery — not a prime + subcontractor stitched together.
- **Velocity operating model** — pre-vetted pods, early tech enablement (SSC infra / ERP sandbox / data platform in M2–M3), overlapping waves.
- **"Transform in Flight"** wave strategy — quick wins *and* systematic redesign, avoiding "lift junk then fix junk."
- **AI factory** as an operating model, not a project — 100+ use cases with a repeatable pipeline and responsible-AI governance (DPDP Act 2023).
- **Exit-ready by design** — mixed teams, KT gates, advisory-only tail.
