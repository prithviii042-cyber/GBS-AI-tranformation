SSC design, process re-engineering, org design & change

Executed as a single integrated engagement with wave-based process transition. We design the future operating model, re-engineer ~85 processes to L5, stand up a 10-tower SSC and manage transition — with 100% clarity on SSC vs Retained vs Corporate accountability and SOX-equivalent audit readiness.

Our point of view

Five design choices that separate a great SSC from an average one

POV 1

Process-led, not lift-and-shift

We digitise and standardise before we transition. Lifting a broken process just relocates the cost — Transform-in-Flight moves it once, clean.

POV 2

A thin retained org

Most "retained" teams quietly re-do the SSC's work. We design the leanest viable retained layer with a hard RACI at the seam — no shadow processing.

POV 3

Standardise 80, localise 20

One global process design, with deliberate variation only where regulation or genuine market difference demands it. Standardisation is what makes automation possible.

POV 4

Automation-first design

We design the to-be process with automation and AI already embedded — not a manual process we automate later. Every L5 is tagged with its automation hooks.

POV 5

Global process owners

End-to-end accountability (O2C, P2P, H2R) sits with a named owner across BUs — killing the hand-off gaps that fragment FMCG operations.

Every process runs through a disposition decision before it moves:

Eliminatenon-value work Simplifycut steps & hand-offs Standardiseone global design AutomateRPA / IDP / AI Digitisezero digital breaks Retainonly where it must

Scar tissue

Our point of view

Five architecture beliefs for a System-Governed enterprise

POV 1

Keep the ERP core clean

Fit-to-standard on an S/4HANA-class core; innovation and custom logic live in side-by-side extensions — so upgrades stay cheap and the core stays fast.

POV 2

Data products, not data dumps

A medallion lakehouse serving governed, owned data products with contracts — the trusted feed an autonomous system depends on. MDM is the foundation, not an afterthought.

POV 3

Right tool for the job

RPA for deterministic, ML for prediction, GenAI for language, agentic for autonomous decisions. Matching the pattern to the problem is what stops "AI slideware."

POV 4

Governance is the platform

Responsible-AI, DPDP and guardrails are built into the platform (gateway, registry, eval harness), not bolted on — the only way autonomy scales safely.

POV 5

Composable & event-driven

API-led connectivity and an event backbone keep the estate real-time and loosely coupled — so use cases plug in without re-wiring the enterprise.
