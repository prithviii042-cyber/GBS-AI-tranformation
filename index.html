04 · Scope of Work

Part A & Part B — click to switch

Our approach mapped to every RFP workstream — A1–A11 (Process, Org, SSC & Change) and B1–B11 (Digital, Data & AI).

◆ Recommendation: Transform in Flight

Best-practice FMCG SSC transformation combines early quick wins with systematic redesign. (1) Lighthouse wave — lift-and-shift simple, high-volume processes to prove the SSC model and lock early savings. (2) Transform waves — transition redesigned processes with automation embedded directly to SSC, so there is no double-move. This avoids "lift junk, then fix junk" while still capturing cost benefits early. RCPL and the bidder jointly sequence the waves during Foundation based on process complexity, automation readiness and BU dependency.
