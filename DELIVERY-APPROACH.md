## 0. Delivery philosophy — the seven operating principles

Everything below is an expression of these. State them up front in the proposal; they *are* the answer to "how is the timeline real?"

1. **Design-first, build-parallel.** All process + architecture design is locked up-front (M0–M4) so build waves never stall on an undecided design. Build (Lighthouse) starts M2 while design continues — overlap is how 24 months becomes 12–15.
2. **One integrated design authority.** Part A (process) and Part B (digital/AI) design in the *same* room against the *same* to-be model. No "throw over the wall."
3. **Transform in Flight.** Lighthouse (lift-and-shift simple/high-volume to prove SSC + lock savings) → Transform waves (redesigned + automated processes moved once). Avoids "lift junk, then fix junk" and double-moves.
4. **Fusion pods, RCPL-embedded.** Every pod is a mixed consultant + RCPL team (40% RCPL FTE) from Day 1 — delivery and capability transfer are the same activity, not sequential.
5. **Thin-slice to production early.** AI and process value ships in weeks, not at the end — first 15–20 AI use cases and 10–20 processes live by M6, proving the model before scale.
6. **Governed velocity.** Weekly integrated PMO + bi-weekly board + single decision authority. Speed comes from *removing decision latency*, not from cutting rigour.
7. **Exit-ready by design.** KT gates, playbooks and a tapering footprint are built in from M0 — the end-state (RCPL runs it alone) is engineered, not hoped for.

## 1. Mobilisation — the first 90 days in detail

The first 90 days decide the whole programme. No ramp-up delay is our biggest single promise, so we show exactly what happens.

### Weeks 0–2 — Stand up the machine
- **Core pods on-site Day 1** — Partner, Engagement Director, Part A & B Leads, AI Factory Lead, Data/ERP Lead, Change Lead — named in CVs, no "TBD."
- **Integrated PMO stood up** — single plan on one tool, RAID log, dependency register, benefits tracker, governance charter signed. Bi-weekly board + weekly PMO cadence live from Week 1.
- **RCPL mixed team named & co-located** — 40% FTE mapped to pods; joint war-room established.
- **Design Authority chartered** — the single cross-stream body that signs off to-be process + architecture together.
- **Access & data rooms** — system access, process documentation, data samples, licence inventory, security clearances initiated.
- **Early tech enablement ordered** — SSC infrastructure, ERP sandbox and data-platform landing zone procurement/provisioning kicked off (target live M2–M3).

### Weeks 2–6 — Diagnose (runs both streams in parallel)
- **Part A diagnostic** — current-state across 5 BUs; process inventory (~60 enabling + 25 value-chain); pain-point & opportunity heatmaps; SSC readiness; baseline cost & FTE.
- **Part B diagnostic** — application/data/AI-maturity assessment; integration landscape; tech pain-points; data-quality baseline; security & DPDP gap scan.
- **Joint output** — a single integrated diagnostic and opportunity map (not two reports), and the **wave-sequencing hypothesis** (which processes/BUs go in which wave, jointly agreed with RCPL).

### Weeks 6–12 — Foundation design + Lighthouse launch
- Target operating model, SSC blueprint (10 towers), enterprise/data/AI reference architecture, ERP target-state direction — all designed against each other.
- **Lighthouse wave launches (~M2)** — 10–20 simple, high-volume processes begin lift-and-shift to prove the SSC model while design continues.
- **AI factory intake opens** — first use cases scoped in Beverages (anchor BU).

**Exit criteria for mobilisation (M3 stage gate):** operating model signed off; SSC blueprint approved; integrated architecture baselined; wave plan locked; Lighthouse live; PMO + governance operating at cadence; early tech environments provisioned.

## 2. The wave model — what moves, when, and why

Four phases, overlapping. Each row shows the *content*, not just the label.

| Phase | Months | Part A (Process/SSC) | Part B (Digital/AI) | Governance/Transfer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Foundation & Design** | M0–M3 | Diagnostic; TOM; SSC blueprint (10 towers); to-be L5 for ALL processes; retained org; SOP/RACI/KPI | Enterprise/data/AI architecture; ERP target state; app rationalisation; 100+ use-case roadmap; investment case | PMO live; board cadence; Design Authority; baseline business case |
| **Lighthouse + Build Wave 1** | M2–M6 | SSC infra live; 10–20 simple processes transitioned (lift-and-shift); service mgmt framework | Data lake + AI platform stood up; **first 15–20 AI use cases in production (Beverages)**; ERP foundation recommended; self-service BI | Wave-1 cutover governance; **KT gate 1 (shadow)**; hypercare |
| **Transform + Scale Wave 2** | M5–M12 | Priority *redesigned* processes (automation embedded) to SSC; scale across Beverages + 2–3 BUs; dashboards live | **AI factory scaled to 50+ in production**; ERP core modules live; executive control tower; RPA/IDP factory | Wave-2 transition; YoY savings tracking; **KT gate 2 (reverse-shadow)** |
| **Complete + Stabilise** | M9–M15 | Remaining BUs; SSC at full scope; 100% SOP/RACI/KPI; steady-state ops | **100+ AI in production**; data lake live to new model; automated KPI + exception mgmt; ERP integrated | Resources hired/trained/optimised; **KT gates 3–4 (RCPL-led → independent)**; consultant → advisory-only |

**Wave-sequencing logic** (jointly agreed at Foundation): a process/BU enters a wave based on (a) complexity, (b) automation readiness, (c) BU dependency, (d) value at stake. Simple/high-volume → Lighthouse. High-value/redesign-heavy → Transform waves. Cross-BU dependencies pull items earlier.

## 3. Part A — detailed method by workstream (A1–A11)

For each: **what we do → how we do it → key output.**

### A1 · Operating Model Design
- **How:** principle-setting workshops with RCPL leadership; define the BU / Corporate / SSC three-layer model; decision-rights mapping (RAPID/RACI); governance model; service-vs-retained boundary rules.
- **Output:** signed-off TOM with accountability model — the anchor every other workstream traces to.

### A2 · Process Architecture (As-Is, L1–L5)
- **How:** APQC-based process taxonomy; process mining on system logs where available + structured workshops; capture ~60 enabling + 25 priority value-chain processes; pain-point, cost, cycle-time and control-gap heatmaps.
- **Output:** As-Is L1–L5 library + opportunity register (the input to prioritisation and the AI use-case funnel).

### A3 · Process Re-engineering (To-Be, L5)
- **How:** design to leading FMCG practice; standardise across BUs; embed **digitisation (zero digital breaks)** and automation/AI at the design stage (not bolted on later); "clean sheet where it pays, standardise everywhere."
- **Output:** To-Be L5 designs for ALL in-scope processes, each tagged with its automation/AI hooks and its target wave.

### A4 · SSC Design & Stand-up
- **How:** service catalogue across **10 towers** (F&A, HR, Procurement, Supply Chain Planning, TMS, Order Management, Pricing, MDM, Analytics, IT Service Desk); operating model per tower; location strategy (GCC vs BPO vs hybrid); technology enablement (workflow, case mgmt, knowledge base); physical/virtual stand-up.
- **Output:** SSC blueprint + live centre, tower by tower, aligned to the wave plan.

### A5 · Retained Organisation Design
- **How:** define what stays in BU/Corporate; roles, structures, capabilities; the BU–SSC–Corporate interface and hand-off protocols.
- **Output:** retained org design with zero-ambiguity interfaces (a scored RFP requirement).

### A6 · Sizing & Talent Strategy
- **How:** activity/driver-based FTE sizing (volume × handling time × automation factor); talent assessment vs future roles; hiring/reallocation plan; GCC/BPO partner strategy; location cost modelling.
- **Output:** FTE glide path + talent plan feeding the business case.

### A7 · SOPs, KPIs, RACI & Controls
- **How:** 100% documented process maps, SOPs, KPIs/KRAs, RACI; internal controls designed into each process.
- **Output:** the operating documentation that makes SSC vs Retained vs Corporate accountability unambiguous.

### A8 · Service Management Framework
- **How:** SLA/OLA design; governance forums; escalation paths; performance dashboards; continuous-improvement (kaizen) mechanism.
- **Output:** run-state service management model.

### A9 · Risk, Compliance & Audit Readiness
- **How:** risk register; control framework; statutory/regulatory mapping; **SOX-equivalent** control readiness; interlock with Part B's DPDP/security work.
- **Output:** audit-ready control environment.

### A10 · Transition & Change Management
- **How:** wave-based transition method — knowledge capture → process documentation → shadow → reverse-shadow → cutover → **hypercare/stabilisation**; stakeholder strategy; comms; role-based training.
- **Output:** each wave transitioned with a controlled cutover and a stabilisation exit gate.

### A11 · Integrated Business Case
- **How:** consolidated YoY savings + investment model, wave-wise; single source of truth reconciled to Volume 2 pricing and A6 FTE glide path.
- **Output:** the business case that must tie out across both volumes.

## 4. Part B — detailed method by workstream (B1–B11)

Part B Phase 1 (Digital Design, M0–M6) is **mandatory** and runs lock-step with Part A Foundation. Phase 2 execution program management is optional and wave-aligned.

### B1 · Digital Strategy & Enterprise Architecture
- **How:** target business/application/data/technology/security architecture, *incorporating SSC and Retained requirements*; architecture governance and standards; digital vision aligned to the TOM.
- **Output:** baselined enterprise architecture — the frame all other B-workstreams design within.

### B2 · Application Landscape Rationalisation
- **How:** current-state app inventory; build-buy-replace decisions; sunset roadmap; alignment to the new operating model.
- **Output:** rationalisation roadmap with sequencing tied to the waves.

### B3 · ERP Modernisation & Process Digitisation
- **How:** ERP target state (S/4HANA or equivalent) aligned to Part A to-be processes; module roadmap; digital workflow design; SSC system requirements. **Foundation recommended by M6; core modules live in Wave 2.**
- **Output:** ERP modernisation plan + phased module go-lives.

### B4 · Data Strategy & Lakehouse Platform
- **How:** data strategy; **Databricks-class lakehouse** design; data products for SSC + Retained; MDM; data governance; quality framework; DPDP-compliant by design.
- **Output:** governed lakehouse — the foundation the AI factory and control tower run on.

### B5 · Analytics, BI & Control Tower
- **How:** self-service BI; **executive control tower** with real-time KPIs; analytics embedded into SSC and BU workflows (not standalone dashboards).
- **Output:** control tower + embedded analytics driving run-state decisions and automated exception management.

### B6 · AI / GenAI / Agentic Strategy & Roadmap  *(see §5 for the factory)*
- **How:** 100+ use-case roadmap across demand, supply, pricing, marketing, finance, HR, customer service; value-sizing; BU sequencing (Beverages anchor); platform + governance design.
- **Output:** the prioritised, value-sized pipeline the factory executes.

### B7 · Intelligent Automation Architecture
- **How:** RPA + IDP + workflow + conversational interfaces; **automation factory** operating model; integration with SSC processes so automation and process transition move together.
- **Output:** automation factory delivering touchless processing into the SSC.

### B8 · Integration & API Strategy
- **How:** integration backbone; API catalogue + governance; event streaming; real-time data movement between ERP, lakehouse, SSC systems and AI services.
- **Output:** the integration layer that keeps the estate real-time and loosely coupled.

### B9 · Cybersecurity, Privacy & AI Governance
- **How:** security architecture; **DPDP Act 2023** compliance; **responsible-AI framework** — model risk, bias testing, audit trail, human-in-the-loop; interlock with A9.
- **Output:** the governance envelope that lets AI scale safely.

### B10 · Tech Operating Model & Talent
- **How:** tech org design for SSC + Retained; capability build; sourcing strategy; vendor management model.
- **Output:** the run-state technology organisation RCPL owns post-M15.

### B11 · Technology Investment Case
- **How:** consolidated TCO/ROI (licences, cloud, platform build, run cost); wave-aligned to Part A; focused on business-KPI and productivity outcomes.
- **Output:** tech investment case, reconciled into the A11 integrated business case.

## 5. The AI factory — operating model in detail (the differentiator)

100+ use cases is not 100 projects. It is a **repeatable pipeline** with an operating model. This is where we win the 15% AI-depth score and reinforce integrated capability.

### 5.1 The pipeline (idea → production → scale)
1. **Intake & ideation** — sourced from the A2/A3 process opportunity register + BU workshops. Every use case is tied to a real to-be process, not invented in isolation.
2. **Prioritisation** — value × feasibility scoring; sequenced by BU (Beverages first) and by data/automation readiness.
3. **Design** — solution pattern chosen: analytical ML, GenAI, or **agentic** (autonomous within guardrails); data, integration and human-in-the-loop points defined.
4. **MVP sprint (2–6 weeks)** — thin-slice to a working, evaluated MVP. **15–20 MVPs in Beverages by M7.**
5. **Validate** — business validation + responsible-AI checks (bias, model risk, DPDP) before production.
6. **Productionise** — deploy on the platform with monitoring, guardrails and rollback.
7. **Scale** — replicate the pattern across BUs; **50+ live by ~M12, 100+ by M15.**
8. **Monitor & improve** — drift, cost, accuracy and value tracked; feedback to the pipeline.

### 5.2 The platform
Lakehouse (B4) → feature store + model registry → **LLM gateway + prompt management + agent orchestration** → evaluation harness → guardrails/policy layer → observability. Automation factory (B7) and control tower (B5) plug into the same backbone (B8).

### 5.3 The pod
**Fusion team:** AI/ML engineer + data engineer + process SME (from Part A) + RCPL member + responsible-AI reviewer. Same pod designs the process *and* the AI — that is why they embed cleanly.

### 5.4 Representative use-case domains
Demand (GenAI sensing, agentic replenishment) · Supply (schedule optimisation, predictive maintenance, IDP) · Pricing (promo-ROI simulation, agentic price monitoring) · Marketing (GenAI content studio, media-mix) · Finance (autonomous close copilot, touchless AP, variance narratives) · HR (screening copilot, policy assistant) · Customer Service (agentic resolution, VoC analytics). *Full value-sized 100+ roadmap in the Part B annexe.*

### 5.5 Responsible AI (non-negotiable, ties to B9)
Model-risk tiering, bias testing, full audit trail, human-in-the-loop for consequential decisions, DPDP-compliant data handling, and an AI governance board. Scale safely or you don't scale.

## 6. Keeping Part A and Part B integrated (the anti-silo mechanism)

Integration is the highest-weighted capability (25%). We engineer it, not assert it:

- **Single Design Authority** signs off process + architecture *together* — no independent sign-offs.
- **Process-to-technology traceability matrix** — every to-be process (A3) links to its ERP module (B3), data product (B4), automation (B7) and AI use case (B6). Nothing designed orphaned.
- **Shared wave plan** — a process and its enabling tech/AI move in the *same* wave.
- **Fusion pods** — process SMEs sit inside AI/automation pods and vice-versa.
- **One integrated PMO plan** — a single dependency register spanning both streams.

## 7. Governance & PMO operating model

| Tier | Cadence | Who | Decides |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Board Steering** | Bi-weekly | RCPL execs + Partner (single decision authority) | Scope, investment, wave gates, escalations |
| **Integrated PMO** | Weekly | Eng. Director + stream/pod leads + RCPL PMO | Plan, dependencies, RAID, benefits |
| **Design Authority** | Weekly | Part A + Part B leads + architects + RCPL | To-be process + architecture sign-off |
| **Pod stand-ups** | Daily/weekly | Fusion pods | Delivery execution |

**Stage gates** at each phase/wave boundary with explicit exit criteria (see §1 for the M3 gate). **RAID + dependency + benefits** all live on one tool with a single source of truth. Speed is manufactured by **removing decision latency** — every decision has a named owner and an SLA.

## 8. Change management & adoption

- **Structured method** (Prosci/ADKAR-style): awareness → desire → knowledge → ability → reinforcement, per stakeholder segment.
- **Change network** — RCPL champions embedded in every BU/tower.
- **Training academy** — role-based curricula for SSC, retained org, and BU users; built once, reused per wave.
- **Communications** — cadence tied to wave milestones; leadership visibility at each cutover.
- **Adoption metrics** — usage, proficiency, and sentiment tracked; reinforcement where adoption lags.

## 9. Capability transfer — the four-gate mechanic

Exit-readiness is engineered from M0. Consultant footprint tapers to **advisory-only post-M15.**

| Gate | When | RCPL role | Test to pass |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gate 1 — Shadow** | ~M6 (Wave 1) | Observes + participates | RCPL team can describe the process/tooling end-to-end |
| **Gate 2 — Reverse-shadow** | ~M9–M12 (Wave 2) | Leads with consultant support | RCPL runs it; consultant only intervenes on exception |
| **Gate 3 — RCPL-led** | ~M12–M14 | Runs independently, consultant on-call | Stable operation for a defined period without intervention |
| **Gate 4 — Independent** | ~M15 | Owns fully | Consultant to advisory-only; all playbooks/tooling handed over |

**Mechanisms:** mixed pods (40% RCPL) from Day 1; SOPs/playbooks/runbooks authored as we go; tooling + platform ownership transferred; the training academy left behind; AI factory intake process handed to RCPL so *they* keep shipping use cases post-M15.

## 11. Delivery risks & mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Design decisions slip → build stalls | Design-first with a hard M3/M4 sign-off gate + single Design Authority with decision SLAs |
| RCPL 40% FTE not available on time | Named + committed at contract; co-location Week 1; escalate resourcing gaps at board |
| Lighthouse becomes "lift junk" | Transform-in-Flight rule: only simple/high-volume in Lighthouse; redesigned processes moved once in Transform waves |
| AI use cases stall at pilot | Factory pipeline with productionisation + platform built early (M2–M3); MVP→production discipline, not endless POCs |
| ERP modernisation drags the timeline | ERP foundation *recommended* by M6, core modules phased into Wave 2 — decoupled from SSC/AI critical path |
| Part A/B drift into silos | Single plan, single Design Authority, traceability matrix, fusion pods |
| Data quality blocks AI/analytics | MDM + quality framework in B4 from Foundation; data readiness is a use-case prioritisation criterion |
| Change fatigue / low adoption | Wave-based change, champion network, role-based academy, adoption metrics with reinforcement |
| Compliance exposure (DPDP/SOX) | B9 + A9 interlocked from M0; responsible-AI + controls designed in, not retrofitted |
